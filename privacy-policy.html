Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This internal tool ("the Tool") is developed and operated by Doron AI Studios for publishing Doron AI Studios-produced content to Doron AI Studios-owned social media accounts on platforms including TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, and YouTube.

Data we handle

The Tool processes API access tokens and video/image content authored by Doron AI Studios, solely for the purpose of publishing that content to Doron AI Studios's own accounts. No data belonging to third parties or end users is collected, stored, or shared by this Tool.

Data we do not collect

The Tool does not collect personal data from TikTok users, viewers, or any third party. It does not track, profile, or store information about anyone other than the Doron AI Studios accounts it publishes to.

Storage

API credentials are stored locally on Doron AI Studios's own equipment and are not transmitted to any party other than the relevant platform's official API (TikTok, Meta, Google/YouTube) for the purpose of publishing content.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact Doron AI Studios through its official channels.
